The Problem

The reproducibility crisis is a trust problem.

A claim doesn't need to be true to spread on social media. It needs to sound scientific. Misinformation spreads by accident; disinformation spreads on purpose; both move faster than the evidence that would settle them. Health myths, wellness marketing, and viral "studies" all travel on the authority of science itself, and by the time anyone checks whether the evidence holds up, the claim has already shaped what people believe, what they buy, or what they refuse to do.

70%

of researchers have failed to reproduce another scientist's experiments. Findings that can't survive a second look don't stay in journals. They end up in headlines, product claims, and social feeds, repeated as fact long after the evidence behind them has collapsed.

$28B

wasted annually in the U.S. on irreproducible preclinical research. The same unverified findings fueling that waste are the ones spreading online with no way to check them, and no authoritative record either side of a dispute can point to.

The Ledger

One record. Open to everyone.

Independent.

DATOM does not produce the claims it verifies, does not employ the authors, and does not profit from the answer. Verification happens at arm's length, every time.

Recorded.

Every verification enters the ledger with the claim, the evidence examined, the result, and a timestamp. The record persists and updates as new evidence arrives.

Public.

Every claim on the ledger is open to check, no account required: what it says, its confidence, and when it was last confirmed. Commissioning a new, in-depth Confidence Report on a specific claim is a paid service; checking the public record of a claim already on the ledger is not.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is DATOM, in one sentence? ⌄
DATOM is an infrastructure for representing scientific evidence as atomic, inspectable units, so confidence in empirical claims can be computed, audited, and updated transparently.
What is a Datom? ⌄
A Datom is an atomic unit of evidence: a specific measurement, method, dataset, assumption, constraint, or replication condition. Datoms cannot be merged or simplified without losing meaning.
What is a Datomer? ⌄
A Datomer is a structured evidence object composed of Datoms that together answer a specific empirical question. Datomers are built from inspectable components, not summaries.
Does DATOM summarize the literature? ⌄
No. DATOM does not summarize, paraphrase, or average research. It preserves evidence at the atomic level so conclusions can be inspected rather than inferred from narrative.
How is confidence determined? ⌄
Confidence is derived from evidence structure (density, replication, agreement, and contradictions), not authority. It updates as evidence changes and is a separate number from the raw direction of the evidence: how many sources support versus contradict a claim.
Why are contradictions included? ⌄
Contradictions are information. They reduce confidence and narrow applicability without erasing supporting evidence or forcing false consensus.
Is a determination an AI opinion? ⌄
Confidence scores are generated by DATOM's pipeline from underlying evidence, not asserted by a human editor. AI assists with extraction and synthesis, but the evidence objects themselves are explicit, referenceable, and auditable: every source behind a determination has a real citation you can check.
Does DATOM decide what is true? ⌄
No. DATOM makes confidence inspectable and computable, but decisions remain with the reader.
How is this different from a meta-analysis? ⌄
Meta-analyses aggregate results. DATOM preserves structure, provenance, and disagreement. Each contribution remains visible and comparable.
Who is this for? ⌄
Anyone who wants to know whether a claim holds up: readers checking something they saw online, researchers, investors performing diligence, and institutions making high-stakes decisions.
Is DATOM free to use? ⌄
Yes. Every determination on the public ledger is free to read. Paid tiers add full archive access, claim requests, and commissioned verification for institutions.
How can my organization work with DATOM? ⌄
Companies, research labs, investors, institutions, government agencies, and universities can commission independent verification or integrate the record directly through DATOM Pro.
Is this replacing peer review? ⌄
No. It complements peer review by making underlying evidence explicit and reusable.
Does DATOM work with proprietary data? ⌄
Yes. DATOM supports private Datomers and internal confidence scoring while maintaining the same structural logic as the public ledger.
How are new studies handled? ⌄
New evidence is added as new Datoms. Confidence updates transparently as the record evolves.
What prevents misuse or cherry-picking? ⌄
DATOM makes cherry-picking visible. Omitting evidence reduces credibility, because the underlying structure is inspectable, not hidden inside a summary.
Is DATOM a database, a product, or a standard? ⌄
Infrastructure: a data model, a confidence framework, and a trust primitive.
Why hasn't this existed before? ⌄
Scientific communication was previously optimized for publication, not computation. DATOM treats evidence as a first-class object.

Responsibility for decisions remains human. We provide the infrastructure to make them defensible.
