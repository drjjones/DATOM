The Problem

The reproducibility crisis is a trust problem.

A claim doesn't need to be true to spread. It needs to sound scientific. Health myths, wellness marketing, and viral "studies" travel on the authority of science itself — and by the time anyone checks whether the evidence holds up, the claim has already shaped what people believe, what they buy, or what they refuse to do.

70%

of researchers have failed to reproduce another scientist's experiments. Findings that can't survive a second look don't stay in journals. They end up in headlines, product claims, and social feeds, repeated as fact long after the evidence behind them has collapsed.

$28B

wasted annually in the U.S. on irreproducible preclinical research. The same unverified findings fueling that waste are the ones circulating publicly with no way to check them, and no authoritative record either side of a dispute can point to.

The Ledger

One record. Open to everyone.

Independent.

DATOM does not produce the claims it verifies, does not employ the authors, and does not profit from the answer. Verification happens at arm's length, every time.

Recorded.

Every verification enters the ledger with the claim, the evidence examined, the result, and a timestamp. The record persists and updates as new evidence arrives.

Public.

This is not a B2B tool with a public-facing veneer. Anyone can look up a claim on the ledger and see that it was verified, by whom, and when, without asking permission. Commissioning a new, in-depth Confidence Report on a specific claim is a paid service; checking the public record of a claim already on the ledger is not.
