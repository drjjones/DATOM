The Problem

The reproducibility crisis is a trust problem.

A claim doesn't need to be true to spread on social media. It needs to sound scientific. Misinformation spreads by accident; disinformation spreads on purpose; both move faster than the evidence that would settle them. Health myths, wellness marketing, and viral "studies" all travel on the authority of science itself, and by the time anyone checks whether the evidence holds up, the claim has already shaped what people believe, what they buy, or what they refuse to do.

70%

of researchers have failed to reproduce another scientist's experiments. Findings that can't survive a second look don't stay in journals. They end up in headlines, product claims, and social feeds, repeated as fact long after the evidence behind them has collapsed.

Baker, Nature 2016 (survey of 1,576 researchers)

$28B

wasted annually in the U.S. on irreproducible preclinical research. The same unverified findings fueling that waste are the ones spreading online with no way to check them, and no authoritative record either side of a dispute can point to.

Freedman et al., PLOS Biology 2015

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is a determination an AI opinion? ⌄
No. The verdict and grades are computed by DATOM's pipeline from the underlying evidence, not asserted by a human editor. AI assists with extraction and synthesis, but the evidence objects themselves are explicit, referenceable, and auditable: every source behind a determination has a real citation you can check.
Does DATOM decide what is true? ⌄
No. DATOM makes the evidence inspectable and computable and records where it points. The decision remains with the reader.
What prevents misuse or cherry-picking? ⌄
DATOM makes cherry-picking visible. The full bibliography is public, so omitting evidence reduces credibility rather than hiding inside a summary. The domain classification and grading basis are printed on every record and are auditable.
Who pays for verification, and doesn't that bias the result? ⌄
Payment moves priority, never outcome. Public demand triggers verification whether or not the claim's subject pays. Commissioning and sponsorship are disclosed on every record. The ledger is append-only, so an unfavorable result can never be withdrawn, only appended to. And public sentiment carries zero weight in the verdict or grades, enforced by a permanent build gate. Read the full firewall policy →
What happens when DATOM gets one wrong? ⌄
The correction is public and the history is preserved. The record is append-only: a correction is added as a new entry with the evidence that triggered it, never rewritten or deleted. You can see when and why a grade or verdict changed, and the version history stays on the record.
Why does this claim have no verdict yet? ⌄
The record is open to every claim, so a claim that has been asked but not yet verified still has an entry: it is on the record and pending, not rejected. Entries advance through continuous prioritization across editorial selection, reader demand, and sponsorship.
How is a claim graded and given a verdict? ⌄
The verdict is the direction of the evidence: how many independent sources support versus contradict the claim. The Evidence Grade is the material quality of that evidence base, read as a letter, alongside a field-relative grade against what is achievable in the claim's domain. Both are computed from the evidence structure, not authority, and both update as evidence changes.
Why are contradictions included? ⌄
Contradictions are information. They move the verdict toward Contested and narrow applicability without erasing supporting evidence or forcing false consensus.
Who is this for? ⌄
Anyone who wants to know whether a claim holds up: readers checking something they saw online, researchers, investors performing diligence, and institutions making high-stakes decisions.
Is DATOM free to use? ⌄
Yes. Every record's verdict, grades, and summary evidence are free to read. A free account opens the full evidence tree. Paid tiers add sponsored verification and commissioned Confidence Reports.
How can my organization work with DATOM? ⌄
Companies, research labs, investors, institutions, government agencies, and universities can commission independent verification or integrate the record directly through DATOM Pro.
Is this replacing peer review? ⌄
No. It complements peer review by making underlying evidence explicit and reusable.
How is this different from a meta-analysis? ⌄
Meta-analyses aggregate results. DATOM preserves structure, provenance, and disagreement. Each contribution remains visible and comparable.
How are new studies handled? ⌄
New evidence is added as new entries. The verdict and grades update transparently as the record evolves, and the change is logged in the version history.
Does DATOM work with proprietary data? ⌄
Yes. DATOM supports private records and internal grading while maintaining the same structural logic as the public ledger.
Is DATOM a database, a product, or a standard? ⌄
Infrastructure: a data model, a grading framework, and a trust primitive.
What is a Datom? ⌄
A Datom is an atomic unit of evidence: a specific measurement, method, dataset, assumption, constraint, or replication condition. Datoms cannot be merged or simplified without losing meaning.
What is a Datomer? ⌄
A Datomer is a structured evidence object composed of Datoms that together answer a specific empirical question. Datomers are built from inspectable components, not summaries.
