DATOM+edu

The program proposes a mark. The teacher decides.

DATOM+edu is DATOM's teaching line. Its first piece is a teaching platform for a science course that runs on the teacher's own computer: grading of scanned and typed work with the teacher in the loop, a gradebook with retakes and a Canvas handoff, a course assistant, and an evidence view of what the class's work shows. The models run on that same machine. Names and student records stay there.

Record shows

How often the program's marks stood, measured per job.

AI cannot be fully trusted in science, and it cannot be fully trusted with a student's grade. That is where the verification layer comes in: the teacher's review is the check, and the platform keeps a measured record of how often the program's marks stood.

What it does

Six panels. One course folder.

Grading

Scanned paper exams and typed work, graded against the teacher's answer key or criteria. The teacher reads each proposed mark on the answer check screen and corrects it in place. Feedback is written for each student and read back against the facts before it goes out.

Gradebook

Course grades by marking period from the grading policy the teacher wrote, with retakes, scores entered by hand, and an export in Canvas's import format. Formative work is practice only and never moves the grade.

Students

Each student's year on one screen, and a progress report for the family.

Evidence

What the class's work shows: by standard, by question, by mistake, and what to reteach. A teaching brief for a teacher working outside their own subject.

Assistant

Ask anything about the course, a grading job, a student, or the gradebook. It answers only from the course record, cites the document for every policy statement, and says when the answer is not in the record.

Department view

A department head sees the evidence and the aggregate, with no student named. Groups under five are dropped. Access is a key the teacher issues and can revoke.

Where the data lives

On the teacher's computer.

  • Scans, rosters, grades and the models stay on the teacher's Mac. There is no account and no cloud copy.
  • Nothing about a student is sent anywhere in the background. What leaves is a file the teacher chose to make: grades for Canvas, a family's progress report, or a counts only summary for the department.
  • The program never writes into Canvas or any gradebook. It makes files the teacher uploads.
  • A grade stays preliminary until the teacher's review is finished.
  • Student names appear only to the teacher. They are never in the department view, in logs, or in any shared file.
  • Every write into the course folder is backed up first and needs the teacher's approval. Delete moves to the Trash; nothing is erased.
  • DATOM+edu never touches DATOM's cloud. It is a separate program on a separate machine.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it send student work to an AI company? ⌄
No. The models run on the teacher's computer. Nothing about a student goes to any online service in the background.
Does the program decide grades? ⌄
No. It proposes a mark with its evidence and how sure it is. The teacher accepts or corrects every one before it counts, and the record shows how often the program's marks stood.
Does it work with Canvas? ⌄
It makes files in Canvas's import format for grades and feedback. The teacher uploads them. It never writes into Canvas.
What does a department head see? ⌄
The evidence view and the aggregate, with no student named and small groups dropped, through a key the teacher issues and can revoke.
Is this the same product as DATOM's ledger? ⌄
No. DATOM reads the evidence behind scientific claims and publishes what it finds. DATOM+edu applies the same position, that AI output is checked before it is trusted, to a teacher's grading, on the teacher's own machine. The two never share data.
