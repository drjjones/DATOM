For Decision Makers

The Maturity Framework

⌄

Founders use DATOM to navigate technical maturity and existential risk with structured evidence.

  • Deployment: Integrate into board updates and milestones to show objective progress.
  • Value: Reduces the risk of premature scaling by identifying pivots before capital is exhausted.
← Past Facing: Accountability

Defend why a path was taken under uncertainty to protect against hindsight bias.

→ Future Facing: Readiness

Map work to readiness bands to identify when to move to full deployment.

  • Deployment: Technical due diligence phase to assess "claim weight."
  • Value: Improves timing for capital injection by reducing asymmetry.
← Past Facing: Auditability

→ Future Facing: Risk Posture

For Government Agencies

The Policy Evidence Base

⌄

Regulators and health agencies use DATOM to check the scientific claims behind guidance, funding decisions, and official communications before they go out.

  • Deployment: Screen the evidence behind proposed regulations, grant applications, or health guidance before it's published.
  • Value: Reduces the risk of basing policy on claims that don't hold up, and gives agencies a defensible record when a decision is reviewed later.
← Past Facing: Accountability

Show the evidence a decision was based on, not just the decision itself, when it's reviewed later.

→ Future Facing: Institutional Trust

Publish the confidence behind a guideline alongside the guideline itself.

For Universities

Campus-Wide Access

⌄

Give students and faculty direct access to DATOM, so evaluating evidence becomes part of how they learn to do research, not a separate skill taught once and forgotten.

  • Deployment: Site-wide or department-wide access so students can check claims in coursework, theses, and their own early research.
  • Value: Builds evidence literacy as a habit, and gives faculty a shared, inspectable standard for what counts as well-supported.
← Past Facing: Teaching

Show students exactly why a claim is or isn't well-supported, not just that it is.

→ Future Facing: Research Readiness

Graduates who already know how to audit evidence before they ever run a lab.
