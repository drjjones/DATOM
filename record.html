Do GLP-1 weight-loss drugs cause you to lose muscle while you lose weight?

DATOM looked at 102 studies that directly tested this claim. Of those, 77 (75%) support the idea that GLP-1 weight-loss drugs cause you to lose muscle while you lose weight; 25 (25%) point the other way. So as the evidence stands today, most of the clear findings back the claim — that is what the “Supported” verdict below means. But the Grade D beside it matters just as much: the evidence base is limited, so this is the current direction of the evidence, not a settled, high-certainty result.

Exact claim checked GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy at clinically approved doses reduces skeletal muscle mass during weight loss in overweight and obese adults, as measured by lean body mass or appendicular skeletal muscle mass over a treatment period of 12 to 68 weeks.
